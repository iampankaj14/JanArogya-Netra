# Hackathon Demo Presentation Guide

This guide defines a 5-7 minute live presentation sequence for the **JanArogya Netra** command center.

---

## ⏱️ Timeline & Presentation Sections

| Time | Section | Narrative | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0:00 - 1:00** | **The Hook & Problem Statement** | District Health Officers lack real-time visibility. Operational crises are only detected after outbreaks occur. | Show **Splash Screen** → Transition to **Login Screen** |
| **1:00 - 2:00** | **The Command Center (Situation Room)** | Netra is a pro-active AI operating system. DHO logs in to view critical alerts. | DHO logs in → Opens **Situation Room** → Point out critical alert notifications |
| **2:00 - 3:15** | **AI Redistribution Decision** | An outbreak is causing a shortage. Netra AI recommends moving surplus stock to prevent a crisis. | Tap **AI Recommendation** → Review reasoning → Click **Approve Transfer** |
| **3:15 - 4:15** | **Visualizing Operations (District Map)** | The transfer is initiated. DHO opens the map to verify stock health levels. | Navigate to **District Map** → Point out pin states changing from Yellow to Green |
| **4:15 - 5:30** | **Scenario Simulation & Forecasting** | "What if the district experiences a heatwave?" We can run simulations to predict resource requirements. | Open **Scenario Simulator** → Select "Heatwave" → Click **Simulate** → Show graph results |
| **5:30 - 6:30** | **Netra Chat & Impact** | Ask Netra general questions. Show final audit reports. | Open **Netra Assistant** → Type: "Do we have surplus ORS?" → Show response → Show **Reports** |
| **6:30 - 7:00** | **Conclusion & Pitch** | Netra is a replicable command center model that can scale to districts across the nation. | Wrap up presentation |

---

## ⚡ Live Demo Walkthrough Steps

### Step 1: App Launch & Authentication
- **Action**: Launch app and show Splash screen. Log in as DHO Rajesh Kumar.
- **Narrative**: *"Welcome to JanArogya Netra. I am logging in as the District Health Officer. Immediately, I am presented with the Situation Room."*

### Step 2: Approve AI redistribution
- **Action**: Tap the **AI Recommendation Card** recommending moving 50 Dengue Kits from Dharampur to Rampur Kalan. Review the reasoning showing a surge in Dengue cases, and click **Approve**.
- **Narrative**: *"Instead of a static chart, Netra AI has already computed a solution: move surplus kits from Dharampur to Rampur Kalan where a dengue outbreak is growing. I approve this recommendation, and the transfer order is dispatched."*

### Step 3: Geographic verification
- **Action**: Navigate to **District Map**. View the map pins.
- **Narrative**: *"By viewing the map, I can verify that the health score pin of Rampur Kalan has updated from Critical Red to Stable Green as resources are en-route."*

### Step 4: Run Scenario Simulation
- **Action**: Open the **Scenario Simulator**, select the "Heatwave" preset, and click **Simulate**. Show the predicted demand for IV fluids.
- **Narrative**: *"Before a crisis occurs, I can run a 'What-If' simulation. Under a projected heatwave, Netra forecasts that IV fluid demand will spike by 150%. This allows us to stockpile resources in advance."*

### Step 5: Conversational Query
- **Action**: Open the **Netra Assistant** chat bubble, send: *"Do we have surplus ORS in the district?"* Show Netra's text response.
- **Narrative**: *"I can query Netra in natural language. It scans our inventory repository and confirms that Dharampur PHC has a surplus of 800 sachets of ORS, ready for redistribution."*
