## Project

-   Name: JanArogya Netra
-   Tagline: *The Eye of Public Healthcare*
-   Competition: Google Cloud Build with AI -- Code for Communities
-   Track: Smart Health
-   Deadline: 5 July

## Vision

JanArogya Netra is **not another healthcare dashboard**. It is an
AI-powered **District Health Intelligence Command Center** that
continuously monitors PHCs/CHCs and recommends actions before
operational problems become crises.

## Core Philosophy

Data → Reasoning → Decision → Action

Never build passive dashboards. Every screen should help an officer make
a decision.

## DHRE

District Health Reasoning Engine.

Responsibilities: - Predictive Intelligence - Diagnostic Intelligence -
Prescriptive Intelligence - Preventive Intelligence

## Major Innovations

### 1. District Health Reasoning Engine

Reasons across all PHCs simultaneously.

### 2. Autonomous Medicine Redistribution

Suggests where medicines should move.

### 3. AI Morning Briefing

Executive summary every morning.

### 4. AI Health Risk Score

Green / Yellow / Red district visualization.

### 5. Situation Room

Priority-first decision interface.

### 6. AI Scenario Simulator

"What if..." simulations.

Examples: - Dengue outbreak - Heatwave - Supply delay - Doctor absence -
Flood - Festival crowd

AI estimates: - Medicine demand - Staff requirement - Bed requirement -
Suggested transfers - Confidence score
